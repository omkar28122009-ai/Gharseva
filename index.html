Need to know

Frequently asked questions

What does the visit charge cover?
The visit charge covers the technician's inspection visit where applicable. Parts, consumables, cleaning, labour or additional repair work may cost extra. Confirm the exact inclusions for your service before approving work.
Will I know the repair cost in advance?
The price shown on a service card is an indicative starting price. The final estimate can depend on the appliance model, issue, parts and work required. Please approve the estimate before additional work begins.
Does adding a service to my cart confirm a booking?
No. Adding to cart only saves your selected services in this browser. Submitting a booking request is also not a confirmed appointment. Our team must check availability and confirm the booking with you.
Can I change or cancel my appointment?
Please contact support as early as possible to request a change or cancellation. Please check the Service notes & policies section below and contact support directly to confirm any applicable terms.
Is repair work covered by a warranty?
Warranty availability and duration depend on the specific service and parts used. Confirm the written warranty terms before work starts. Inspection or visit charges may not be covered.
How do I book more than one service?
Add each service to your cart, review the estimated total and select “Proceed to booking”. Your cart is saved in this browser on this device; all included appliance categories are available on this page.
Clear terms, before you book

Service notes & policies.

Please review the basics before confirming any repair or maintenance work.

Pricing & service scope
Prices shown are starting estimates based on the service descriptions. Visit charges, parts, consumables, additional labour and the final scope may differ. Ask for a written estimate and approve extra work before it begins.
Booking & cancellation
Submitting a request does not reserve a confirmed appointment. Availability, changes and cancellations must be confirmed directly with support. Please contact support as early as possible if your plans change.
Privacy & contact details
This single-file demo stores the cart and a local copy of booking requests in your browser. When you continue to WhatsApp, the details in the request are included in a prefilled message for you to review and send. Do not submit information you do not wish to share.
Parts & warranty
Warranty terms depend on the approved work and parts used and should be provided in writing. Cleaning, inspection and visit charges do not automatically include a repair warranty. Parts and work outside the stated scope may cost extra.
